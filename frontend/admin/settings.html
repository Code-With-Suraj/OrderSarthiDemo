How In-Store QR Ordering Works:
  1. Customer enters your shop & scans the QR with any phone camera.
  2. Your store catalog opens instantly in In-Store Shopping Mode.
  3. Customer selects items and places the order for instant counter pickup.
  4. If the customer does not have a smartphone, open ⚡ POS Billing to create the order for them and deduct stock instantly.
